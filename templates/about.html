Our Story

PhoVite was born from a simple idea: your special moments deserve invitations that are as unique as the events themselves.

Traditional invitation makers force you to choose from generic templates, while custom design services are expensive and time-consuming. We asked ourselves: what if AI could transform your personal photos into stunning, professional-grade invitations in seconds?

That's exactly what we built. Using state-of-the-art generative AI, PhoVite seamlessly blends your photos with artistic styles to create magic.

Our Mission

"To democratize professional event design by making it accessible, affordable, and instant for everyone. We believe that every celebration—big or small—deserves an invitation that wows guests and sets the perfect tone for your special day."
